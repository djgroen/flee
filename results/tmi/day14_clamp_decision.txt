REGIME JUSTIFICATION (the reason; misfit is the confirmation):
  The clamp (conflict_intensity > 0.9 -> movechance >= 0.95) was tuned for
  Fukushima, whose hazard is a LOCALISED SPATIAL spike: only the inner zone
  crosses the threshold, so a near-certain-flight floor is physically right
  THERE. TMI's threat is UNIFORM and information-driven (conflicts.csv gives
  every town the same c(t); only t=10 exceeds 0.9). The clamp therefore fires
  POPULATION-WIDE, imposing a town-blind 0.95 movechance on all 12 towns at
  the advisory spike. That is the wrong physics for an information regime, and
  it is exactly why (criterion 1) total evac becomes flat & saturated near the
  ceiling under clamp ON (range [0.36,0.38],
  span 0.018) -- CMC is unidentifiable on the evac level. With the
  clamp OFF, CMC recovers its identifiability (span 0.082; Day 13
  total-evac ST 0.61 -> 0.78). DISABLE is regime-correct AND restores
  calibratability.

RETUNE vs DISABLE: a threshold retune (e.g. firing only above a SPATIAL
  conflict-gradient, not a uniform level) would also avoid the population-wide
  floor, but at TMI there is NO spatial gradient to key off -- the spike is
  uniform -- so any level-based threshold either fires everywhere or nowhere.
  DISABLE is therefore the clean choice for this regime (no representative
  threshold exists).
