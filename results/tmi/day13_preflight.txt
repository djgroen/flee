0.5  BASE-SAMPLE CONVENTION + TIMING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"n" in Day 7b = SALib base sample N (n_samples), NOT total evaluations.
Day 13: base N = 64 (power of two, recommended); calc_second_order=True ->
        total samples = N*(2D+2) = 64*10 = 640.
ABM noise control: each sample = MEAN QoI over 3 fixed seeds [0,1,2].
        total model runs (clamp ON)  = 640 * 3 = 1920
        total model runs (clamp OFF) = 640 * 3 = 1920   (Task C, same design)
Peak timestep: t = 10 (c=1.0).
c(t) > 0.9: ONLY at t = 10 (c[8]=0.35, c[9]=0.75, c[11]=0.85). The clamp fires
        on a single timestep — so "fraction departed by t=8" is safely pre-clamp,
        and only QoIs measured at/after t=10 are clamp-exposed.
