0.7  TOTAL-EVAC QoI DENOMINATOR (must match check 26 for the flip to be valid)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The established check-26 QoI (run_tmi_day12_first_run.per_run_metrics) is:
    total_evac_rate_20mi = evac_frac_town * town_pop / POP_20MI
where evac_frac_town = (modelled town agents that reached a camp) / (all agents),
town_pop = 258000 (sum of all 12 town pops), POP_20MI = 663500. The model only
represents ~258k of the 20-mi region's 663.5k residents as MOBILE town agents;
the QoI scales the modelled evac fraction by that population ratio (~0.389). Day
14 reuses this EXACT definition so check 41 is the precise flip of check 26.
(Zone evac rates are plain fractions of zone agents, compared directly to the
Zeigler 0.55/0.50/0.16 percentages.)

EXPECTATION: Day-13 clamp-OFF evals show total_evac is CMC-identifiable (ST=0.78).
At the locked beta the modelled evac FRACTION floors near ~0.54 at CMC~0 (clamp
OFF); scaled by 0.389 that is ~0.21 -> in the [0.15,0.30] band. The System-2
term (P_S2*sigma, ~0.5 under TMI's uniform threat) supplies that floor even at
CMC=0 (control: ORIGINAL mode, P_S2=0, CMC=0 gives evac_frac 0.16). The OPEN
QUESTION at preflight is whether the per-ZONE gradient also matches; the camp-
adjacency confound (0.5) is expected to inflate the outer zone. Guardrail: if the
SCALED total cannot enter [0.15,0.30] under either clamp state, STOP & REPORT.
